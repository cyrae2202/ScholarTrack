# ScholarTrack

Discover, save, and track educational opportunities with AI.

## Overview

Students often miss valuable opportunities because information is scattered across websites, social media posts, school announcements, and community groups. Scholarships, competitions, internships, workshops, exchange programs, and summer programs all have different deadlines, requirements, and application processes.

ScholarTrack helps students discover opportunities that match their interests, save the ones they care about, and stay organized throughout the application journey.

Rather than acting as another task manager, ScholarTrack combines AI-powered opportunity discovery with application tracking, making it easier for students to explore possibilities and take action before deadlines pass.

---

## The Problem

Students face three major challenges:

### Discovering Opportunities

Educational opportunities are spread across countless websites and platforms. Many students simply never hear about programs that could benefit them.

### Staying Organized

After finding an opportunity, students often lose track of deadlines, requirements, and application progress.

### Managing Multiple Applications

Students frequently apply to several opportunities at the same time, making it difficult to monitor their progress and plan effectively.

---

## Our Solution

ScholarTrack provides a centralized platform where students can:

* Discover opportunities using AI-powered search
* Save opportunities for later review
* Track application progress
* Monitor upcoming deadlines
* Organize opportunities by status and category
* Build a personal opportunity pipeline

The platform is designed to help students move from discovery to action.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Opportunity Discovery

Students can describe their interests, goals, or academic background, and ScholarTrack helps identify relevant opportunities.

Examples:

* Scholarships
* Competitions
* Internships
* Workshops
* Summer Programs
* Exchange Programs

### Opportunity Tracking

Save opportunities and manage them through different stages:

* Saved
* Planning
* Applying
* Submitted
* Accepted
* Rejected

### Deadline Monitoring

ScholarTrack highlights important deadlines and helps students stay ahead of upcoming application dates.

### Search and Filtering

Quickly find opportunities using:

* Keyword search
* Opportunity type filters
* Status filters
* Sorting options

### Dashboard

A personalized dashboard provides an overview of:

* Saved opportunities
* Active applications
* Upcoming deadlines
* Application progress

### Google Authentication

Simple sign-in with Google for a fast onboarding experience.
