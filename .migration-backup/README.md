# ScholarTrack

## Tagline

Track opportunities. Never miss a deadline. Shape your future.

## Project Overview

ScholarTrack is a platform that helps students discover, organize, and manage educational and career opportunities in one centralized workspace.

Students often miss valuable opportunities such as scholarships, competitions, internships, summer programs, workshops, and leadership initiatives because information is scattered across multiple websites, social media platforms, and online communities. Managing deadlines, requirements, and application progress can quickly become overwhelming.

ScholarTrack addresses this challenge by providing a simple and effective system for tracking opportunities, monitoring deadlines, and staying organized throughout the application process.

## Problem Statement

Students face several challenges when pursuing opportunities:

* Information is scattered across different platforms.
* Important deadlines are easily missed.
* Application requirements are difficult to organize.
* Managing multiple applications at the same time can be overwhelming.
* Students often do not know which opportunities best match their interests and goals.

These challenges can prevent students from accessing experiences that could significantly impact their academic and professional development.

## Solution

ScholarTrack provides a personalized dashboard where students can save opportunities, organize application information, track progress, and receive reminders for upcoming deadlines.

The platform enables students to take control of their opportunity pipeline and stay focused on the actions needed to achieve their goals.

## Core Features

### Opportunity Management

Create and organize opportunities including scholarships, competitions, internships, workshops, and summer programs.

### Deadline Monitoring

Track application deadlines with countdown timers and deadline alerts.

### Application Progress Tracking

Monitor application status through stages such as:

* Saved
* Planning
* Applying
* Submitted
* Accepted
* Rejected

### Personalized Dashboard

View upcoming deadlines, active applications, and progress statistics in one place.

### Smart Opportunity Matching

Recommend opportunities based on user interests, academic fields, and goals.

## Target Audience

* Middle school students
* High school students
* University students
* Scholarship seekers
* Competition participants
* Internship applicants

## Impact

ScholarTrack helps students stay organized, improve productivity, and make better decisions regarding educational and career opportunities. By reducing missed deadlines and simplifying opportunity management, the platform empowers students to pursue experiences that can shape their future.
